Privacy Policy

Star Rock Puzzle · Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Controller & Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how Star Rock Puzzle handles player information. For any privacy questions, technical support, or data requests, please contact us directly at:

Support & Privacy Email: star-rock-puzzle@googlegroups.com

2. Information We Handle

The game is built to operate with the minimum amount of information required:

  • Basic account data (optional, via Google Play Games Services): if you choose to sign in with your Google account, we only receive your Play Games player identifier and your public profile name (Display Name) to identify you in-game and on leaderboards.
  • Game progress & preferences: completed levels, best scores/moves, earned gold, Altar offerings, unlocked achievements, owned cosmetics, and local settings (sound, music, vibration).

3. Purpose of Processing

The data listed above is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your player profile through Google Play Games Services.
  • Save and synchronize your game progress in the cloud (Saved Games) across devices.
  • Record unlocked achievements and scores on the game's official leaderboards.
  • Respond to support requests voluntarily sent via email.

We do not collect passwords, financial details, precise location, or contacts, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. How Your Information Is Protected

Communication between the app and Google Play Games Services takes place over encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS) managed by Google's official infrastructure. Local progress is stored in the app's private storage on your device, and cloud backups are protected under your Google account's security controls.

5. Support & Data Deletion

You can request support or deletion of your associated data at any time:

  • By email: write to star-rock-puzzle@googlegroups.com stating your request (support or data deletion) and the details needed to locate your inquiry.
  • From your Google account: you can revoke app access and delete cloud-saved data from your Google Play Games privacy settings on your device or Google account.
  • Local data: you can clear app storage or uninstall Star Rock Puzzle from your device to erase locally saved data.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update game features or connected services, we will publish the revised policy on this page with an updated revision date.
