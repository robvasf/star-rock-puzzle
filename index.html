A swipe, a plan, a perfect solve

Star Rock Puzzle is a portrait sliding puzzle set in pixel-art temples. Swipe a stone and it travels until a wall or another stone stops it. Line up the Star Rocks with their sockets, use neutral stones to set up your next move, and open the temple chest when the board is solved.

200 puzzles across eight temples

Start with guided lessons, then work through eight themed stretches of the campaign, from the Ancient Temple and Mossy Garden to the Volcanic Caldera and Stellar Crown. Early boards teach you to use walls as stopping points. Later boards ask you to coordinate several Star Rocks and neutral stones.

Replay completed levels from the level selector, beat your best move counts, and earn gold stars by finding optimal routes.

Four Secret Worlds with living walls

Finish the campaign to uncover four more worlds, each with 200 puzzles. Rain courses along the stone, ice ripples over frozen walls, flames crawl around the corridors, and fog rolls through the masonry. Close-ups in the clip show the same wall effects playing in-game.

Endless Mode

Clear all four Secret Worlds to unlock a run that keeps counting upward. A pool of 2,000 boards feeds the mode, and its wall effects, temple backdrops, and HUD borders change as you advance. See how far you can climb on the Endless leaderboard when Google Play Games is available.

Treasure, cosmetics, and the Offering Altar

Earn gold from temple chests and spend it in the Star Shop to customize your avatar, Star Rocks, sockets, and neutral stones. After the campaign, make offerings at the altar to rise through its ranks and build your Offering score. 27 achievements track milestones from your first clear to the deepest challenges.

Play at your own pace

Play in portrait with one-swipe controls, restart a board to try another route, and adjust sound effects, music, ambience, and vibration independently. The soundtrack features 14 original 16-bit themes. Play offline with local saves. With optional Google Play Games sign-in on a compatible Android installation, you can sync progress and use achievements and the Offering and Endless leaderboards.

What is the game about?

In Star Rock Puzzle, each level is an enclosed sanctuary where rocks slide until they hit walls or other blocks.

  • 200 campaign levels with progressive difficulty and secret worlds.
  • Endless Mode to test your puzzle-solving skills without limits.
  • Offering Altar & Relics to unlock visual themes for rocks, sockets, and avatars.

Google Sign-In

The game optionally integrates Google Play Games Services so you never lose your progress when switching devices:

  • Cloud Save: syncs completed levels, gold, cosmetics, and achievements.
  • Achievements & Leaderboards: records your milestones and Altar offering score.
  • Offline Play: if you prefer not to sign in, your progress is stored locally on your device.

Transparency & Data Handled

We respect your privacy and follow data minimization principles. When you sign in with Google, Star Rock Puzzle only accesses the Google Play Games services needed for:

Public player display name Game progress (Cloud Save) Achievements & leaderboards
