What is the game about?

In Star Rock Puzzle, each level is an enclosed sanctuary where rocks slide until they hit walls or other blocks.

  • 200 campaign levels with progressive difficulty and secret worlds.
  • Endless Mode to test your puzzle-solving skills without limits.
  • Offering Altar & Relics to unlock visual themes for rocks, sockets, and avatars.

Google Sign-In

The game optionally integrates Google Play Games Services so you never lose your progress when switching devices:

  • Cloud Save: syncs completed levels, gold, cosmetics, and achievements.
  • Achievements & Leaderboards: records your milestones and Altar offering score.
  • Offline Play: if you prefer not to sign in, your progress is stored locally on your device.

Transparency & Data Handled

We respect your privacy and follow data minimization principles. When you sign in with Google, Star Rock Puzzle only accesses the Google Play Games services needed for:

Public player display name Game progress (Cloud Save) Achievements & leaderboards

We do not sell personal data or show intrusive ads. Read full details in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, or contact us at star-rock-puzzle@googlegroups.com.
